## הפרומפט (העתק מכאן)

A screen recording of a real business automation running by itself, at
night. Dark UI throughout, deep violet-to-black background, crimson-pink
accent (#FF2E63). Hebrew right-to-left interface. No people, no faces, no
narration, no talking head. Calm, precise, documentary - like watching a
machine work, not an advert.

Shot as one continuous screen capture with soft cuts between five beats.
A small clock in the corner advances through the sequence and is the only
thing that tells you time is passing.

BEAT 1 - 23:47. A website contact form on a dark page. Hebrew fields.
A submission lands: the name field reads "רונית לוי", the message field
reads "רוצה הצעת מחיר לשיפוץ מטבח, דחוף להשבוע". A submit button pulses
once in crimson-pink and the form clears.

BEAT 2 - 23:47. A processing panel. Two labels resolve into place one
after the other, in a pink-to-violet gradient pill: "הצעת מחיר" then
"דחיפות גבוהה". A thin progress line sweeps left to right beneath them.
Hold two seconds.

BEAT 3 - 23:48. A WhatsApp-style chat on dark. An outgoing green message
bubble types itself out in Hebrew, right-aligned:
"היי רונית, קיבלתי את הפנייה לגבי שיפוץ המטבח. כדי לתת הצעה מדויקת - מה
גודל המטבח ומה בדיוק מתחלף?" Two grey ticks appear, then turn blue.

BEAT 4 - 23:48. A spreadsheet on dark. Four Hebrew column headers: שם,
נושא, דחיפות, מקור. A new row writes itself cell by cell - רונית לוי /
הצעת מחיר / גבוהה / אתר - and flashes soft green once as it commits.

BEAT 5 - 08:00. Morning. A task card on dark with a small avatar:
"לחזור לרונית לוי - הצעת מחיר", and beneath it in smaller grey text
"נפתחה בלילה · שובצה לצוות מכירות". The cursor never touches it.

CLOSE - the screen dims and one Hebrew line fades up, centred, heavy
sans-serif, white on near-black: "כל זה קרה בזמן שישנת."

Style: crisp UI motion, ease-out transitions, subtle glow on the accent
colour, faint film grain. No zoom-heavy camera moves, no swooshes, no
stock-video look, no 3D robots, no neon circuit-board imagery.
