# פרומפט לסרטון הראש - תרשים זרימה שנבנה מעצמו

הפרומפט באנגלית כי כל מודלי הווידאו עובדים טוב יותר באנגלית.
הצבעים לקוחים מהמשתנים האמיתיים של האתר (`assets/css/site.css`).

---

## Prompt

A dark-mode automation builder canvas, seen perfectly straight on, filling the
frame. On a near-black background (#0A0613) with a faint grid of dots, five
rounded rectangular nodes build themselves one after another from left to
right, joined by smooth curved connector lines that draw themselves like ink
flowing along a path.

Each node is a small dark card (#14091F) with a thin light border, a rounded
square icon tile on its left filled with a magenta-to-violet gradient (#FF2E63
to #8B31D8), and one short white label in a clean geometric sans-serif.

Timing, evenly spaced: node one fades in and settles; a curve draws to the
right and node two appears; then node three, four and five the same way. Once
all five are placed, a bright magenta pulse of light travels the whole path
from the first node to the last, and each node glows briefly as the pulse
passes through it. A soft violet and magenta bloom drifts slowly behind the
canvas.

Style: sleek modern SaaS product interface, crisp flat vector shapes, subtle
glow, high contrast, precise and calm. Locked-off camera, dead straight on, no
perspective, no movement of any kind.

Colors: background #0A0613 and #140B23, node surface #14091F, borders white at
13% opacity, accent gradient #FF2E63 to #8B31D8, connector lines #FF2E63 fading
into #2F6BFF, labels pure white, secondary text #C6BCD8.

On-screen text - render exactly these five labels, correctly spelled, and no
other text anywhere in the frame:

  New lead
  Read and classify
  Send reply
  Add to sheet
  Notify team

The first and last frames are identical so the clip loops seamlessly.
